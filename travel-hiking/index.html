Trail Tips for Beginners

🚶

Start Small

Choose trails rated "easy" first. Build endurance over weeks before tackling difficult terrain.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least half a litre of water per hour on the trail. Never rely on finding water in the wild.

🌎

Navigation

Download offline maps before you go. Cell signal disappears fast once you're deep in nature.

🧨

Leave No Trace

Pack out all your rubbish. Stay on marked paths to protect fragile ecosystems.
